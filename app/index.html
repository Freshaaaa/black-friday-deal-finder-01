How we check a deal

Three rules, applied to every card above. If a deal fails one, the card says so.

1. Read the price where it is sold

Prices come from the retailer's own Black Friday release or a deal report that names the store and the price seen. Nothing is invented, rounded up, or carried over from last year.

2. Compare against the usual price

The verdict uses the discount and the dollars saved. A 9% cut on a bundle is not a bargain just because it says Black Friday on it.

3. Say when the store hides the was-price

Some stores show a sale price with no regular price at all. Those deals are labelled No was-price instead of getting a discount we cannot verify.
